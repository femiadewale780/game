THE RULES ARE SIMPLE

Four is the magic number.

  1. 01
    Bring a friend.

    Create a room and share the code. Your friend joins on their own device.

  2. 02
    Take your turn.

    Choose a column. Your piece falls to the lowest empty space. The creator goes first in round one.

  3. 03
    Connect four.

    Get four in a row across, down, or diagonally to win. A full board with no winner is a draw.

Want another round? Both players choose Play again. The starting player alternates.
